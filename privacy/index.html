Privacy Policy

DokuFun is designed to work without an account and to keep your game information on your device.

Effective date: 1 September 2026

At a glance

  • No DokuFun account
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or developer-operated tracking
  • Game data stays on your device
  • Optional purchases use Google Play Billing

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Toufic Elmir, publishing under the developer name and brand Elmir Productions, handles information in the DokuFun mobile application (“DokuFun”, “the app”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Toufic Elmir is the app publisher, privacy contact and data controller where that concept applies; Elmir Productions is the public developer brand, not a separate legal entity. This policy applies to the Android version of DokuFun distributed through Google Play.

Questions about this policy can be sent to Toufic Elmir at t.elmir@elmirolives.com.

2. Information the app handles

DokuFun stores information locally on your device so the game can operate and remember your choices. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • current puzzle boards, notes, elapsed time, mistakes and saved progress;
  • completed puzzles, best times, statistics, local leaderboard entries and achievements;
  • hint use, hint balances, coin balances and unlocked puzzle content;
  • purchase entitlements and product identifiers needed to make purchased content available; and
  • app preferences such as theme, accessibility, sound and haptic settings.

This information is stored using your device’s local app storage. Elmir Productions does not operate a DokuFun server that receives or stores this game information.

3. What DokuFun does not do

DokuFun does not require or provide a user account. The app does not contain advertising and does not use developer-operated analytics, behavioural profiling or tracking technologies. It does not request your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or health information.

4. Optional purchases and Google Play

Optional digital purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google may process information such as your Google account, device, payment method and transaction details under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Elmir Productions does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

DokuFun receives purchase status and purchase identifiers from Google Play so it can unlock content, restore eligible non-consumable purchases and prevent duplicate fulfilment. In the current version, those records are used and retained locally on your device; Elmir Productions does not operate a purchase-verification server that receives them.

Consumable virtual coins are device-local and may not be recoverable after you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Eligible non-consumable purchases may be restorable through Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase.

To learn how Google handles information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Why local information is used

The locally stored information described above is used only to provide app functionality: saving and resuming games, applying your settings, calculating game statistics, managing hints and coins, showing achievements, and making purchased content available.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is overwritten through normal app use or removed. You can delete it through your Android device’s settings by clearing DokuFun’s storage, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently remove progress, settings and unspent virtual coins.

Because Elmir Productions does not hold this information on a DokuFun server, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely for you.

7. Security

DokuFun relies on Android’s application storage protections and Google Play’s purchase services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, but the app is designed to minimise privacy risk by keeping game information on your device and by not operating a developer data server.

8. Age suitability

DokuFun is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Elmir Productions, contact us so we can investigate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DokuFun’s features, privacy practices or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be posted at this address with a revised effective date. Material changes will also be communicated in the app or through its store listing where appropriate.
